About this program

External training starts the fire; internal champions keep it burning. This program selects and develops your own people into certified Claude champions — equipped with our curriculum, coaching techniques and refresh cadence.

It's how adoption survives attrition, reorgs and the next model release: capability that lives inside your organisation, not in a vendor's calendar.

What's included

  • Champion selection framework and cohort design
  • Deep-dive training beyond the standard curriculum
  • Teach-back practice with live coaching
  • Ready-to-run internal workshop materials
  • Certification and quarterly refreshers

Outcomes you can expect

  • A certified internal training bench
  • Self-sustaining adoption rhythm
  • Lower long-run enablement cost
  • A durable AI-first culture

Who it's for: L&D teams and organisations building durable in-house AI capability.
