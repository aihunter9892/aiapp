Frequently asked questions

What is Claude AI training?
It's hands-on corporate training that gets your team using Anthropic's Claude on real work — prompting, Claude Projects, Claude Code and AI agents — mapped to your industry and tools. We deliver it on-site or online, from a 2-hour masterclass to a 2-day bootcamp.
Who is this for?
Leadership teams, marketing, sales, product, engineering, operations, finance and HR. We tailor every cohort by role and seniority so the examples are relevant.
Where do you deliver?
Across India — including Mumbai and every major metro — and in 40+ countries worldwide, on-site or live online.
