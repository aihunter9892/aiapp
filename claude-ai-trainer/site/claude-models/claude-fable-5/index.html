What Claude Fable 5 is good at

Claude Fable 5 opens Anthropic's Claude 5 generation — a new Mythos-class tier that sits above Opus in capability. It is the most intelligent Claude model generally available, built for the work where depth of reasoning genuinely changes the outcome.

In training we position Fable 5 as the summit of the escalation path: Haiku for speed, Sonnet as the daily driver, Opus for hard problems — and Fable 5 when the task deserves the frontier. Teams learn to spend that capability where it moves the needle.
