About this program

Two hours that change how a room thinks about AI. The Claude Masterclass is a live, demo-driven session that shows exactly what Claude can do on real business tasks — and hands the audience a prompting method they can use the same afternoon.

It's the ideal opener: energising for an offsite or town hall, and the proven first step before a deeper corporate programme.

What's included

  • Live demos on tasks from your industry
  • The TAGS-style contextual prompting method, taught simply
  • Interactive audience exercises
  • Q&A tuned to your organisation's questions
  • Take-home quick-reference prompt guide

Outcomes you can expect

  • A shared, accurate mental model of Claude
  • Immediate hands-on confidence
  • Energy and pull for deeper adoption
  • A clear next-step roadmap

Who it's for: All-hands audiences, offsites, leadership town halls, conferences.
