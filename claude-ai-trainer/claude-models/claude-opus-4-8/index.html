What Claude Opus 4.8 is good at

Claude Opus 4.8 is the frontier of the Claude line — the model we reach for when a task needs genuine reasoning depth: untangling a messy dataset, driving a multi-tool agent to a finish, or refactoring across a real codebase.

In training we show teams exactly where Opus earns its cost and where a lighter model is the smarter call — so you spend on capability only where it moves the needle.

Best for

  • Complex, multi-step reasoning and analysis
  • Long-horizon agentic workflows and tool use
  • Hard software engineering and large-codebase work
  • High-stakes drafting where quality beats cost
